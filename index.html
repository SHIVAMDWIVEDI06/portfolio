About

Faridabad, India

I'm an AI engineer. I work on how language models are trained to behave, and on machines that have to make their own decisions out in the field.

Right now I'm an LLM intern at Ethara AI, fine-tuning domain-specific models and building the validation pipelines that check their output for reasoning, safety and accuracy before it goes anywhere.

Before that I spent a summer running RLHF and SFT pipelines on frontier models and writing the adversarial prompts that find where they break.

Away from language models I build hardware: an agricultural rover that identifies crop disease on-board, and a forecasting system for grid energy storage. I'm finishing a B.Tech in Computer Science (AI & ML) at K.R. Mangalam University.

A short flight through a world I built for this page, written in plain WebGL with no libraries. It starts over open ground and passes each of my projects. Each project has its own station you can open. An air taxi picks you up, flies you into the city, and climbs the tower floor by floor, stopping at each one so you can look around. About a minute if you let it run.
